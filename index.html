About Us

Welcome to Discover Your Self (DYS), a transformative educational digital initiative dedicated to guiding individuals toward purposeful living, mental clarity, and spiritual fulfillment through the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita As It Is.

Operating online via https://dys-gray.vercel.app and conducting sessions both virtually and physically at ISKCON NVCC Pune, Discover Your Self serves as a modern bridge between ancient Vedic philosophy and contemporary life challenges.

🌟 Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower students, youth, working professionals, families, and self-development seekers with practical life principles and spiritual processes from the Bhagavad Gita As It is—including the sublime path of Bhakti Yoga (Loving Devotional Service)—enabling them to master the mind, overcome stress, build strong moral character, and discover their true inner potential.

Our Vision

To cultivate a conscious, values-driven generation grounded in the principles of the Bhagavad Gita, equipped with emotional resilience, spiritual intelligence, and a deep sense of purpose to lead a balanced, joyful, and impactful life.
